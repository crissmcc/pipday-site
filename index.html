Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 14, 2026

Privacy at a Glance

Pipday processes your selected photos and creates your stickers directly on your device. Your photos and sticker images are not uploaded to Pipday servers or to our analytics provider.

Pipday does not require an account.

Pipday uses Amplitude to collect limited analytics data about how the app is used. This includes a pseudonymous device identifier, app interaction events, session information, app and system version information, device model, and device language.

Pipday does not use advertising identifiers, cross-app tracking, targeted advertising, or precise or coarse location data.

1. Photos and Sticker Creation

When you choose a photo to create a sticker, Pipday uses Apple’s system photo picker so you can select the specific photo you want to use.

Pipday does not request access to browse your entire photo library through this process.

The selected photo is processed on your device to create your sticker. Pipday does not upload your selected photos or sticker images to its own servers.

Pipday does not store the original photo file selected from your photo library. The sticker images and related information needed by Pipday are stored locally on your device.

2. Data Stored on Your Device

Pipday stores information needed to provide its features locally on your device. This may include:

  • stickers you create;
  • sticker thumbnails and rendered images;
  • creation dates and selected sticker styles;
  • color palette information;
  • information needed for Pipday to remember your stickers and app state.

Pipday does not currently provide cloud synchronization or its own user account system.

3. Saving to Photos

If you choose Save to Photos, Pipday may request permission to add images to your photo library.

This permission is used only to save the image you requested. Pipday does not use this permission to browse or collect your existing photo library.

Images that you save to Photos are managed by your device and the Photos app. Deleting a sticker from Pipday does not delete copies that you have already saved to Photos.

4. Purchases

Pipday may offer purchases through Apple’s App Store using StoreKit.

Purchases and payment information are processed by Apple. Pipday does not receive or store your payment card information.

Pipday receives the information needed from Apple to determine whether paid features should be unlocked.

For more information about how Apple handles purchase information, please refer to Apple’s privacy policies.

5. Support and Feedback

If you choose to contact Pipday through Share Your Thoughts, your message is sent using your device’s mail system.

The email may include:

  • your email address;
  • the content of your message;
  • your Pipday app version;
  • your iOS version;
  • your device model.

The app version, iOS version, and device model are shown in the email before you send it.

This information is used to respond to your request, investigate problems, and improve Pipday.

Support emails are processed by the email services used to send and receive them and are kept only for as long as reasonably necessary for support purposes.

You may contact us at any time to request deletion of personal information you previously sent through a support message.

6. Sharing and External Services

Pipday allows you to share stickers, share Pipday, open Instagram, visit the App Store, or leave a rating.

When you choose to use one of these features, information may be handled by Apple or by the third-party service you choose to use. Their own privacy policies apply to information handled by those services.

Pipday does not control how external services process information after you choose to interact with them.

7. Analytics, Advertising and Tracking

Pipday uses Amplitude, a third-party analytics service, to understand how people use the app and to help improve its features and reliability.

Pipday sends a limited set of analytics information to Amplitude, including:

  • a pseudonymous device identifier used to distinguish devices and app usage over time;
  • session information;
  • interactions with Pipday features, such as creating and saving stickers, opening sticker details, downloading or sharing stickers, viewing the purchase screen, and interacting with purchase controls;
  • Pipday app version;
  • iOS version;
  • device model;
  • device language; and
  • technical event information such as event timestamps.

Pipday does not send your selected photos, sticker images, color palette contents, support messages, or payment card information to Amplitude.

Pipday does not set an Amplitude user account identifier and does not require a Pipday account.

Pipday configures Amplitude not to collect location information for analytics. IP address is not stored as an analytics property or used by Pipday to derive country, region, city, or other location information.

Pipday does not use IDFA or other advertising identifiers.

Analytics information is used only to understand app usage, evaluate features, identify product issues, and improve Pipday.

Pipday does not use analytics information for targeted advertising or cross-app tracking and does not sell your personal information.

8. Deleting Your Data

You can delete stickers from within Pipday. When you delete a sticker, Pipday removes the corresponding locally stored data used by the app.

Images that you previously saved to Photos or shared with another service are not deleted automatically.

Pipday does not currently provide an account or cloud synchronization service. Removing Pipday from your device removes its local app data, although copies stored elsewhere, such as images saved to Photos or device backups, may remain.

Analytics events that have already been sent to Amplitude are not automatically deleted when you delete a sticker or remove Pipday from your device. These analytics records use a pseudonymous device identifier rather than a Pipday account or your name or email address.

If you have questions about analytics data or would like to make a privacy-related request, you may contact us using the email address below.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Pipday’s features or data practices change.

When the policy is updated, the Last updated date at the top of this page will also be updated.
